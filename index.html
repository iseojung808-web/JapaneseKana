Verbs and their forms

These aren't three separate words to memorize — 見みる (miru), 見みてる (miteru), and 見みてね (mitene) are the same verb: the dictionary form, a casual "is watching," and a casual "watch it, okay?" Learn the pattern once and it applies to almost every verb below.

Other words

Nouns and adjectives don't conjugate this way, but kanji still always gets furigana.
